Let’s talk today

Over two cups of tea, share what makes you happy—and the bold dreams you dare to dream

Q1 Two Kings

Do you ever feel there are two kings in your heart? If so, which one is stronger in your life right now? How do the different kings in our lives shape our choices and the battle within?

The king in your heart decides the road you walk and the peace within.

  • Which two “kings” often struggle for control in your life?
  • When you let Jesus the King reign, how does your choice change?
  • The last time your heart was divided, which “king” did you let rule?
Q2 The Battle Within

How should we face and handle the conflicts and battles within us? Share one recent thing you are battling with.

The battle is not shame — it is the beginning of bringing your heart honestly before God.

  • When the inner conflict is at its peak, who is the first person you think of telling?
  • What is different between bringing the battle to God and carrying it alone?
  • What happened with the recent thing you were battling with?
Q3 Heaven & Hell

Using the parable of the rebellious child who rejects his parents’ love, how can we help people understand this difficulty — “God is loving, yet those who reject Him fall into pain/hell”?

It is not God who makes people suffer; rejecting love keeps them in pain.

  • Where does the child’s pain come from in the parable?
  • Is the parents’ “not holding back” cruelty, or another kind of love?
  • Do we know people who are tasting a foretaste of hell?
Q4 Five Words

After learning the differences between “the Valley of Hinnom,” “Tartarus,” “Hades,” “Paradise,” and “the Lake of Fire,” what new clarity does this bring to your understanding of judgment after death and God’s salvation?

When you see the words clearly, you see what God came to save.

  • Which of these words gave you a new understanding of “hell”?
  • Paradise and the Lake of Fire — whose destinations are they?
  • With these differences clear, how do you see God’s judgment and salvation?
Q5 The Kingdom of God

Jesus said, “the kingdom of God is within you.” How can you truly experience the peace of God’s reign and the reality of the kingdom in daily life or in loving one another in the church?

The kingdom of God is not far away; it reigns within you.

  • When do you most strongly feel that God is in control?
  • Which moment of loving one another in church felt like heaven to you?
  • Where can you make the kingdom a little more real today?
Q6 Forgiveness

Living in hatred and bitterness is like “tasting a foretaste of hell.” Facing hurts in relationships, how can we take the first step of forgiveness through the love of Christ?

Forgiveness is not forgetting; it is handing judgment over to love.

  • Are you willing to tell God the bitterness in your heart first?
  • How did Christ forgive us first?
  • How small can the first step of forgiveness be?
Q7 About Healing

In your heart, who do you think God heals, and why do you think God heals us?

Every healed life is proof of God's love for us.

  • Do you think God's healing has "conditions"?
  • Does God heal us because we deserve it, or because of His mercy?
  • As you recall healings you have heard or seen, what touches you most?
Q8 About Testimony

After we are healed, why should we give testimony? When you hear or see someone else being healed by God, how do you feel, and what do you think?

Testimony is not our own credit, but giving glory to the Healer.

  • How does giving testimony help us ourselves?
  • Has hearing others' testimonies ever ignited your faith?
  • If you were to share with one person what God has done in your life, what would you say?
Q9 About pushing the boat out

When you are weary and weak, like Peter who toiled all night and caught nothing, are you still willing to push the boat out for Jesus? Tell us how you feel.

After a night of catching nothing, Jesus still invites you to push out once more into the deep.

  • Have you ever had a time of toiling all night and catching nothing?
  • When Jesus asks you to try again, are you willing, or hesitant?
  • What does pushing the boat out for Jesus mean to you?
Q10 About the call

When you think of Jesus teaching Peter and the others to fish twice, when these two similar events overlap in your memory, how do you feel inside?

Two similar deep waters are Jesus’ unchanging call of love and patience.

  • What details do you remember about these two fishing events?
  • In these two events, how was Jesus’ response to Peter different?
  • When the two scenes overlap in your heart, what comes to mind first?
Q11 About dreams

If you had more money than you could ever spend, what would you most want to do?

Money that isn’t spent is just a string of digits—what would you trade it for?

  • Forget reality for a moment—what’s the first thing you’d do?
  • On whom would you spend it, to change whose life?
  • Is there something you’ve long dreamed of but never got around to doing?
Q12 About happiness

Share the happiest thing
that happened to you lately?

Happiness often hides in the smallest things; the moment you say it out loud, it shines even brighter.

  • What was the last thing that made you laugh out loud?
  • Where did it happen, and who was there?
  • If you had to describe that feeling in one word, which would you choose?
