Introduction

When we speak of the five loaves and two fish, we usually think of experiencing supernatural multiplication and provision! This passage testifies that we as Christians can apply it in our financial giving. Yet what we want to talk about today is the connection between the blessing of the five loaves and two fish and evangelism.

I. Healing Testimonies Draw People to Follow

Scripture · John 6:1-3

After this Jesus went away to the other side of the Sea of Galilee, which is the Sea of Tiberias. And a large crowd was following him, because they saw the signs that he was doing on the sick. Jesus went up on the mountain, and there he sat down with his disciples.

The testimony of healing caused many people to begin following Jesus. We too need to think about how Mustard Seed Church can connect with the blessing of Jesus’ healing.

In our church we have experienced a great deal of healing, yet it seems we have not continuously and systematically manifested the healing power of Jesus.

So we have these experiences, but we have not turned them into the power to evangelize. When Jesus ministered, He taught, preached the gospel, and healed people.

We too need to teach people in every gathering—that is the main function of preaching. We also need to use the time of our gatherings to invite people to come and know the gospel.

II. The Battle in Evangelism

Several brothers and sisters among us have shared how they feel troubled when relatives or friends come to visit on Sunday. The church teaches us not to stop gathering, yet the social obligations between relatives are already engraved in the blood of most of our people. So we feel a battle.

But every time you feel that battle, it is not meant to crush you—God is using these battles to bless you and make you an overcomer.

Yet often we Christians do not become overcomers, because our eyes are fixed on people and we battle against flesh and blood. The Bible teaches us to pray at all times and to fight with prayer.

When you remember that you are not fighting against flesh and blood, but against the evil spirits in the air, you will know that your enemy is not people but the devil.

You cannot drive out demons with violence and shouting. Only by relying on the authority of prayer that Jesus Christ has given you can you fight against the evil spirits in the air! The object of your battle has never been the people around you, but the demons in the air.

We say that every battle is an opportunity God gives us, because God wants us to break through, while the devil wants us to be discouraged and despairing. So you will find that your battles repeat themselves. Fixing your eyes on the negative will discourage you; fixing your eyes on God will make you grateful and move toward breakthrough.

None of this is easy, but do not give up. Giving up is the easiest choice, but it produces no value. Press on—your perseverance will eventually blossom into beautiful flowers.

III. Seize Every Opportunity

We say that the battle is God’s opportunity for us to become overcomers. For example, when you feel the pressure between keeping the Lord’s Day and family obligations because relatives are visiting on the weekend, you can change your perspective: book a meal near the church, invite your relatives to the service, and enjoy a meal and chat together afterward. Doesn’t life become richer?

I encourage the brothers and sisters: even if no one comes to your home on the weekend, you can still be a hospitable person—invite relatives and friends to a meal or coffee near the Hilton on the weekend. Then inviting them to church becomes easier. Seize every opportunity to share the gospel, and ask the Holy Spirit to give us wisdom to do so.

The church is the body of Christ. The church needs teaching, evangelism, and healing. When many brothers and sisters think of the church, they think of the pastor—but the church is all of us. The pastor teaches, brothers and sisters invite friends and relatives to participate, and we pray for and heal people in our gatherings. Aren’t we then living out Jesus’ way of ministry: teaching, preaching the gospel, and healing the sick?

Jesus could have done everything alone, but we are the body of Jesus Christ. When we do what Jesus does, Jesus’ presence is surely with us. It is no longer I who live, but Jesus working in us.

Furthermore, although Jesus could have done everything alone, He still gave the disciples authority and power, letting them work together in partnership to do what they had been given to do. And today we too are Jesus’ disciples; we are to do what Jesus commanded His disciples to do—preach the gospel, heal the sick, and make disciples, following Jesus and doing the same things.

IV. Offer Up Your Five Loaves and Two Fish

Scripture · John 6:4-5

Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was at hand. Lifting up his eyes, then, and seeing that a large crowd was coming toward him, Jesus said to Philip, “Where are we to buy bread, so that these people may eat?”

Jesus tested the disciples by asking them to supply the needs of the crowd. The Scripture says, “He Himself knew what He was going to do.” Today Jesus knows how to supply people’s needs, but Jesus says: what do you think should be done to supply these needs?

Dear brothers and sisters, do not be troubled in heart! Jesus does not expect us to be the providers—He is the true Provider. If we can do anything, it is all by the grace of Jesus! Do not make yourself the source just because you did something! Jesus alone is the source of living water!

I only need to offer up my five loaves and two fish to Jesus like that boy did, and Jesus can display His great power. I believe that boy was not the only one who brought food that day; others may have had food too. But what was not offered up, what was not given, could never become the vessel of a miracle! What you offer up and give will one day bless more people!

Scripture · John 6:12-15

When they had eaten their fill, he told his disciples, “Gather up the leftover fragments, that nothing may be lost.” So they gathered them up and filled twelve baskets with fragments from the five barley loaves left by those who had eaten. When the people saw the sign that he had done, they said, “This is indeed the Prophet who is to come into the world!” Perceiving then that they were about to come and take him by force to make him king, Jesus withdrew again to the mountain by himself.

Man’s offering (the five loaves and two fish) plus God’s great power brings supernatural provision. When it comes to evangelism, our invitations to friends and relatives are the five loaves and two fish we can offer up. And God’s great power is this: we entrust our loved ones into God’s hands, and the moment they accept Jesus, God’s power accomplishes His work in the small, limited portion we have offered!

Closing · Believe That You Have Received It, and It Will Be Yours

I encourage the brothers and sisters: pray for your relatives and friends. Make a list, then pray for them every day, declaring that they will be moved by the Holy Spirit and turn to Jesus. You will always find an opportunity to invite someone to a meal near the Hilton, and then invite them to come early and join a church gathering! Believe that you have received it, and it will be yours. Amen!
