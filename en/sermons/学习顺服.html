Introduction

This past April, the church gathered its co-workers to study dream-streaming together. In the final lesson, the Holy Spirit moved me to become an intercessor for Zhuzhou—for the revival and unity of the Zhuzhou church.

I remember being moved to tears by the Holy Spirit, yet another voice inside said, “You can never become an intercessor. The standard is so high; you fall far too short.” So when Pastor Xiaodong invited the brothers and sisters who were moved to come forward, I hesitated.

Thank God! The Holy Spirit’s work is greater than Satan’s lies, and I still bravely stepped forward to receive the pastor’s blessing. That day, May 5, happened to be Lixia—the beginning of summer. On that day God placed this calling in my heart, making the day suddenly very special. I sensed that a fiery season was about to come!

Then Brother Xiaodong suggested that I share during a Sunday service. Honestly, the proposal surprised me—but what surprised me even more was that I agreed, and agreed without a moment of hesitation. That is why we have today’s sharing. Though three months have passed in between, I thank God for being considerate of my weakness and giving me ample time to prepare, and I thank God for the grace to share my experience and testimony!

The theme of my sharing today is “Learning to Obey.” I came to Mustard Seed Church in September 2022, and it has been nearly four years now. Looking back over these four years, the greatest lesson God has taught me is learning to obey.

I. What Is Obedience?

Before I knew God, I was a very proud person. In the dictionary of my life, the word “obedience” seemed never to exist. I only knew that it was hard to meet anyone I could truly respect. I was rebellious before my parents, and my relationship with my father was not good, because my father’s way of raising children was “spare the rod, spoil the child.” I did not communicate much with my parents; I liked to rely on myself for many things. So my personality was often like a boy’s, strong-willed and distinctive.

After stumbling through half a lifetime in the world on my own, I finally received the gospel and was baptized as a Christian in 2018. I am deeply grateful that God took in someone like me. But back then I still did not know what “obedience” really meant. I simply thought: I want to become an obedient child—obey God’s words!

Later I came to understand that obedience is more than “doing what you are told.” Obedience is the surrender of our inner will combined with action—it is “yielding in the heart” plus “acting in practice.” It also means willingly letting go of our own insistence and gladly submitting to God’s authority and order: first obeying God, and then obeying the various authorities He has established.

Scripture · John 14:15

If you love me, you will keep my commandments.

This says that if we truly love God, we will obey His commandments with a willing heart. It is not forced submission; it is voluntary surrender out of reverence for God. We know that God loved us first—it is not that we loved Him, and then He loved us. “We love because He first loved us” (1 John 4:19). His love is complete. Even when you are very imperfect, God still loves you unconditionally and completely. Amen?

What enables us to obey is the love of Christ; because of His love we are willing to obey. In her preaching, Pastor Joyce Meyer once said: God does not want us to obey in order to buy His love; He wants us to obey Him because we trust Him. He wants us to believe that everything He asks us to do—or not to do—is for our good. So if we love God, we should obey and keep His word.

II. The Experience of Obeying God

At the end of September 2025, we attended the Fire Conference in Hong Kong, and I was deeply touched by the Holy Spirit. God encouraged me again and again through the Scripture verses on the cards, and spoke to me through the conference speakers’ messages. I felt very clearly that God wanted me to do something I deeply did not want to do.

After returning home, I shut myself in the house for two days (because I did not understand why God would ask me to do such a thing), praying continually and seeking God’s will. Finally, when I was willing to obey His will, God opened my eyes and let me understand that His love is beyond measure. He wanted to change the way I saw people and to forgive completely, so that my life could be lifted up.

The moment I received God’s heart, I felt a huge relief. The thing that had seemed extremely hard and unwanted suddenly became simple, and step by step, under the Holy Spirit’s leading, I completed God’s will. This also gave me great confidence, and made me believe all the more that what God intends to accomplish will surely be accomplished!

III. Obeying People Is Harder

Obeying God should not be too hard for us as Christians, because we revere God and know His great power. But obeying people may not be so easy. Why do I say this? Because each of us has different views and different ways of doing things. We have our own ideas, and it is hard to let go of our own insistence.

Scripture · Philippians 2:3

In humility count others more significant than yourselves.

Everyone has a proud heart; we always think we are right, that others’ ideas are incorrect and their ways are not as wise—no one yields to anyone. Only such a person can obey others—one who, in humility, counts others more significant than himself.

First we obey God, and then we obey the authorities He has established. The authority I want to talk about today is the pastor of the church, whom God has established. In other words, God wants us to obey our pastors, and God will bless us through the authorities He has set up.

Scripture · Hebrews 13:17

Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they are keeping watch over your souls, as those who will have to give an account. Let them do this with joy and not with groaning, for that would be of no advantage to you.

The pastors of the church are those who keep watch over our souls and will one day give an account to God. They are God’s faithful servants. But when I first came to Mustard Seed Church, I did not understand this.

As a sister, I had more fellowship with the pastor’s wife. When she corrected me, resentment would rise in my heart. I would even feel that she was deliberately making things difficult, or that her demands were too high and too harsh. In short, all kinds of negative emotions drifted through my mind.

Looking back now, my life truly had many gaps at that time. I did not understand much truth, and I lacked a humble, teachable heart—and that allowed the enemy to damage the heart of unity between us.

IV. The Turning Point of Learning to Obey

Around the second half of 2023, I woke up from a dream one day and clearly remembered a voice in the dream saying to me: “To serve, you must obey!” It was repeated several times. I knew God was speaking to me—to serve, one must begin with obedience.

So I began to humble myself, learning to obey the pastor’s wife’s teaching, learning to honor my pastors, and learning to love. “Better is open rebuke than hidden love.” Now I increasingly love being corrected by my pastors, and I have tasted the sweetness that comes after correction. I can face my own shortcomings with peace.

Closing · Obedience Brings Blessing

Brothers and sisters, perhaps we will spend our whole lives learning to obey. The long process may be hard, but you must believe: as long as you have a willing heart, God will help you and strengthen you, and your life will be continually renewed and shaped.

Let us walk together on this blessed road, because obedience surely brings blessing! May God bless you all! This is my sharing. Thank you, everyone!
