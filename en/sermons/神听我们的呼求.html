Introduction

Dear brothers and sisters, peace to you! Today we will learn a theme together: God hears our cry. I believe many brothers and sisters have experienced the joy of having your prayers heard by God! When you pray about something important and God answers you very clearly, are you not filled with joy? Dear brothers and sisters, if you have experienced God answering your prayer, please wave your hand! Let those who just raised their hands respond: when God heard your prayer, were you not overjoyed? Indeed, when our prayers are heard by God, we become very happy and joyful! But I also know well that some of our brothers and sisters have experienced the pain, helplessness and confusion of praying earnestly about things they care about deeply without receiving an answer! It is as if all your prayers have been spoken into the air!

Truly, when our prayers are answered by God, we are overjoyed, but when our prayers are not answered, especially when the things we care about most receive no answer, deep inside we feel so helpless and so pained!

If you too have experienced that helplessness and pain of praying with no answer, as if speaking into the air, I believe you surely want to find an answer: how should we pray so that God will listen? Some people pray in confusion, unsure whether God will hear them! When they are heard, they rejoice; when not, they are disappointed, as if playing the lottery! Some even reach the point where they no longer expect anything from prayer. If you find yourself in this situation, I invite you to search with me in today's sermon for the answer God gives you! If you are still learning how to pray, I hope you too will gain something from today's sermon! If you are already very skilled at praying, then I invite you to pray with a heart of prayer and stand watch over today's message! Together let us look from the Bible's perspective at how to make your prayers receive God's answer!

Dear brothers and sisters, shall we bow our heads and pray together? Please pray for this time of preaching! Ask the Holy Spirit of God to move among us, ask God to send mighty warring angels between us to fight for our gathering, and to remove every obstacle of the enemy, taking away everything lofty that exalts itself against the knowledge of God! Remove every lie and scheme of the enemy, and grant us humble, teachable hearts, so that as we listen we may lay ourselves down, open our hearts, and receive what God brings to our lives through today's pulpit! (Prayer)

Clear the Prayer Channel

1. Shopping on Taobao needs the internet; prayer needs a connection to heaven's internet, like a phone and a broadcast signal

Dear brothers and sisters, I want to ask you a question: how many of you have bought things on Taobao? Please wave your hand! (Smile) You may wonder what this question has to do with prayer? Let me ask another question and perhaps you will understand! Dear brothers and sisters, if we had no way to get online, could we still go and shop online by ourselves? We all know there is an invisible channel between us and Taobao called the internet; without connecting to the internet, there is no way to shop on Taobao. In the same way, there is also a channel between you and God; you could call it heaven's internet. When you are not connected to heaven's internet, your prayers cannot reach God, and your prayers may receive no answer at all!

2. What are the obstacles that block our connection to heaven's internet?

The Bible tells us in John 9:31 that God does not listen to sinners! Even Psalm 66:18 says, If I had cherished sin in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. That is to say, even if it is only sin in our hearts, if our hearts are defiled, the Bible says the Lord will not hear our prayers. For everyone living in this world, all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God (Romans 3:23). Especially when we did not yet know Jesus or believe in Him, the channel between us and God was solid, completely blocked! Dear brothers and sisters, it is normal that God does not listen to the prayers of sinners, but we are people who believe that Jesus is the Christ. In Christ Jesus, in God's beloved Son, we have redemption and the forgiveness of sins (Colossians 1:14). Brothers and sisters, through the blood of Jesus we have been set free from the curse that God does not hear prayer! So the channel between us and God is now open. Although Psalm 66:18 says, If I had cherished sin in my heart, the Lord would not have listened, Psalm 66:19 immediately continues: But God has surely listened; He has heard my prayer. Dear brothers and sisters, in the past, because we did not believe in Jesus, God did not hear our prayers, but today the curse that God does not hear prayer has been broken by the cross of Jesus Christ! Yet the question today is: are we allowing some obstacles to remain between us and God? You know that without the internet you cannot shop on Taobao. In the same way, if we allow the channel between God and us to be blocked, we will be like someone without internet who cannot use Taobao: our prayers cannot reach God.

So what can block the channel between us and God? First, sin. Therefore James 5:16 says, Therefore confess your sins to each other and pray for each other so that you may be healed. The prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective. Confession opens up the obstacles between us and God! Confession heals and restores our relationship with God! So we must confess our sins to God; and if we have wronged others, we also need to confess to them. Matthew 5:23-25 says, Therefore, if you are offering your gift at the altar and there remember that your brother has something against you, leave your gift there in front of the altar. First go and be reconciled to your brother; then come and offer your gift. Settle matters quickly with your adversary who is taking you to court. Do it while you are still together on the way, or your adversary may hand you over to the judge, and the judge may hand you over to the officer, and you may be thrown into prison. From these two verses we see that if we wrong others and do not admit our fault and be reconciled, we are easily accused, and our spirit suffers loss. And if others have wronged us, we also need to forgive them. Mark 11:25 says, And when you stand praying, if you hold anything against anyone, forgive them, so that your Father in heaven may forgive you your sins. We need to forgive those who have hurt us; otherwise, carrying hurt and the bitterness of unforgiveness, we cannot come before God in prayer with boldness and confidence!

Doubt also becomes an obstacle that blocks the channel between us and God. Because of doubt, Peter, who had been walking on the sea, fell into the water (Matthew 14:25-31). James 1:6-7 says, But when you ask, you must believe and not doubt, because the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea, blown and tossed by the wind. That person should not expect to receive anything from the Lord. If you want your prayer to receive God's answer, you need to remove doubt from your life, because doubt also becomes an obstacle to our prayers.

James 4:3 says, When you ask, you do not receive, because you ask with wrong motives, that you may spend what you get on your pleasures. Waste and pleasure-seeking also become obstacles to our prayers. The Bible clearly tells us that asking wrongly, wasting on pleasures, causes us to ask and not receive.

In general, the hidden sins in our lives, sins we habitually refuse to confess to God, sins we refuse to repent of; when we wrong others by our mistakes and are unwilling to be reconciled; when others hurt us and we are unwilling to choose forgiveness; whenever such situations arise, our relationship with God is blocked. These obstacles are not caused by God, but by ourselves.

Our doubts (James 1:6-7), our pleasures (James 4:3), our pride (1 Peter 5:5), our self-righteousness (Luke 18:9-14), our failure to honor our family members (1 Peter 3:7, wives) — these strongholds in our lives become obstacles between us and God. These are the obstacles we need to remove from our lives. People often ask whether they can practice yoga for exercise? (Can your daughter go to a bar instead?)

3. Clear away the obstacles on heaven's internet channel

(Demonstration: carrying a pile of bags symbolizing doubt, vanity, pride, and self-righteousness, you cannot pass through a narrow passage.)

Today, one situation many of us can hardly bear is having a phone but no battery, or having electricity but no network! Dear brothers and sisters, what about our relationship with God? The network channel of prayer, though invisible, truly exists. Are you willing to clear away the garbage between you and God so that you and God can connect smoothly? You can choose: network connected, or disconnected. Dear brothers and sisters, only when the channel between each of us and God is cleaned can our prayers flow freely! Just as we throw away the garbage in our daily lives so that we can live pleasantly, so tell the person next to you: to pray pleasantly, remove the garbage from your life!

Have Faith in God's Word

Brothers and sisters, we have shared the first point, clearing the prayer channel. Now let us share the second point: have faith that comes from God's Word. Tell the person next to you: have faith that comes from God's Word.

1. Let God's Word dwell in your heart (John 15:7)

Let us look together: John 15:7 says, If you remain in me and my words remain in you, ask whatever you wish, and it will be done for you. The Bible says, if we remain in the Lord and His words remain in us, whatever we wish we will receive when we ask! Brothers and sisters, we must read God's Word often! Only then can God's Word dwell in us! Tell the person next to you: you must read God's Word! And we also need to walk often in God's Word, that is, to obey the Word of God we read. In other words, we must become people who do not follow the desires of our flesh, but who follow the Holy Spirit and submit to the Word of God (Romans 8)! Only then will whatever we wish be granted when we ask!

2. Believe that God will hear our prayers (Matthew 21:22, Mark 11:23-24)

Let us look at one passage: Matthew 21:22 says, If you believe, you will receive whatever you ask for in prayer. From this passage we can see that believing in your prayer is very important; our faith is a very important factor in whether prayer receives an answer.

True faith includes confession with the mouth and belief in the heart, and only both confession with the mouth and belief in the heart determine whether we have true faith. If we cannot confess with our mouths, saying we believe in our hearts is not enough. And if you confess with your mouth but do not believe in your heart, that also is not enough, because the principle of faith here is not addition or subtraction, but multiplication.

Some Christians, under the influence of wrong ideas, do not even know whether God will hear their prayers, and so their prayer becomes formalistic. When people are bound by wrong ideas, they may even stop expecting anything from prayer, or feel confused about prayer. In such a case, our prayers become very weak and powerless!

There is a story about a place that had been dry for a long time. Many Christians gathered at church to pray, asking God to send rain to relieve the drought of the land. After the prayer ended, as everyone prepared to go home, a heavy rain suddenly began to fall, and everyone realized that none of them seemed to have brought an umbrella. Then, out of the church came a little girl holding a small red umbrella. Brothers and sisters, do you think believing that God will hear our prayers happens when we see God's answer, or when we do not yet see it? John 20:29 says, Jesus told him, Because you have seen me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed. Tell the person next to you: blessed are those who believe without seeing!

Many times, we only believe with our minds that the words of the Bible will happen in our lives; it is hard for us to believe from our hearts that the blessings of the Bible will come upon us. Yet we are very willing to believe the things of this world.

For example, your father says, Son (daughter), I have transferred money into your bank card. Have you received the money? But do you believe that when you go to the bank to withdraw money, there really is money in your card?

Hebrews 11:1 says, Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see. Brothers and sisters, we truly need this kind of faith in spiritual things. Faith is the confidence of things hoped for; sometimes we say we have no foundation in our hearts. But the Bible says faith is the solid foundation of the very thing you have been hoping for! Brothers and sisters, is there something in your heart that you have been praying for and expecting to happen? If there is, please wave your hand; God wants you to believe, God wants you to have a foundation in your heart! When you pray, whether you have a foundation in your heart is not written on your face! But in the spiritual world, whether the faith within you truly has a foundation is known by God at every moment!

3. Faith comes from God's Word (Romans 10:17)

Dear brothers and sisters, do you know? We say we must have faith, but where does faith come from? Many people say, I believe! I believe! But do you really know what it means to believe? Not everyone who shouts I believe is a person of faith! You must understand this. Not everything sold in KFC is chicken and fries! Now they also sell youtiao and soy milk! Dear brothers and sisters, do not become people who only say with their mouths that they have faith!

Dear brothers and sisters, Romans 10:17 says, Consequently, faith comes from hearing the message, and the message is heard through the word about Christ. Faith comes from the words of Christ! The more our lives love God's Word, the greater the proportion of faith within us! If our faith is insufficient, it is because God's Word within us is insufficient! Only when we have God's Word can we possibly have faith! Faith that is separated from God's Word is like a flower in a mirror or the moon in the water; it disappears at any moment!

Dear brothers and sisters, do you remember the story of Peter walking on the sea? When the disciples thought they saw a ghost, Jesus said, It is I. Peter said, Lord, if it is you, tell me to come to you on the water. The Lord said, Come! Dear brothers and sisters, Peter was able to walk on the sea because of the words the Lord spoke: Peter, come! The words of the Lord Jesus became the bridge that supported Peter as he walked on the sea. That bridge was Peter's faith for walking on the sea!

I once read in a book that two sisters went out to preach the gospel and passed by a river where the bridge had been submerged by floodwater. The two sisters said to each other, Peter walked on the sea; let us also walk on the water! We are preachers of the gospel; we believe Jesus will also say to us, Walk across! Guess whether these two sisters walked across the water successfully? Brothers and sisters, Peter had Jesus' promise to him, but these two sisters were relying on their own thoughts. That is to say, the basis of their faith was their own mental ideas. Just now we said, where does faith come from? Faith comes from God's Word; these two zealous sisters' faith came from their own minds, and obviously these two zealous sisters were swept away by the flood! So do not take our own thoughts as the voice of God! Tell the person next to you: do not mistake your own thoughts for the voice of God! Of course, God sometimes speaks through our hearts with a small voice! The voice of God rises up suddenly within us! It is not dependent on rational analysis, but a sudden inspiration leaping into our minds. As for sudden inspirations, we still need to discern whether they come from God. If we have another opportunity, we will talk about how to listen to the voice of God; let us continue with the topic of prayer.

Pray According to God's Will

Let us look at one passage of Scripture: 1 John 5:13-15 says, I write these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God so that you may know that you have eternal life. This is the confidence we have in approaching God: that if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us. And if we know that he hears us, whatever we ask, we know that we have what we asked of him.

1. God's will and prayer

What is God's will? God's will is God's Word in the Bible! It includes God's commands! The words God wants people to obey, and the various promises God gives people, are all God's will! Just now we spoke of how the faith God gives comes from God's Word! That is to say, God's Word can build our faith, and God's Word also shows us God's heart! Therefore, a truly faithful person will certainly choose to live in God's Word, and at the same time live in God's will! When we drift away from God's Word, we lose the faith that comes from God and also stray from God's will! When we stray from God's Word and God's will, we likewise become confused in prayer!

2. The story of Balaam

In the Old Testament book of Numbers there was a prophet named Balaam. When Israel was about to enter Canaan and passed through the land of foreigners, the foreigners fought against Israel, and the Lord delivered the foreigners into Israel's hands. When Israel camped near the Moabites, there were some ancient wandering minstrels, people who loved to write songs and sing! Some of them sang, Moab, woe to you! You are destroyed; your men flee, and your women are captured! Then Moab became anxious and fearful, so they asked Balaam the prophet to curse Israel! God said to Balaam, You must not curse Israel, nor go to the Moabites! Balaam told the people of Moab, I will not go with you! The king of Moab sent people a second time, saying, I will bestow great honor on you; whatever you ask, I will give you, only come and curse Israel for me! Balaam said, Stay here tonight, and let me ask God once more!

Dear brothers and sisters, when Balaam asked God the first time, did God give Balaam an answer? God had already given Balaam an answer, so why did Balaam ask God again? The reason is that Balaam wanted to gain something from the Moabites! Dear brothers and sisters, flee from the temptations the enemy of God brings to you! Let our eyes be fixed solely on the Lord Jesus Christ. When the temptations of the enemy come to entice us, our best approach is not to say, Oh, depart from me; oh God, help me not to sin! Rather, flee from temptation! Together with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart, pursue the things of heaven (2 Timothy 2:22).

Keep Praying

Scripture · Luke 11:9–10

So I say to you: Ask and it will be given to you; seek and you will find; knock and the door will be opened to you. For everyone who asks receives; the one who seeks finds; and to the one who knocks, the door will be opened.

1. The widow and the unjust judge (Luke 18:1-8)

Dear brothers and sisters, let us look together at Luke 18:1-8, the story of the widow and the unjust judge. It says there was a judge in a certain town who neither feared God nor cared what people thought. In that town there was an old widow who had been wronged and came to this judge to get justice. The judge was very unwilling to grant justice to a widow! So the widow went to the judge every day crying out for justice! Again and again! The judge thought to himself, I do not fear God nor care about these poor people! But this widow keeps bothering me, so I will see that she gets justice, so that she will not come back and keep bothering me!

Dear brothers and sisters, you have heard what this unjust judge said: he would grant the widow justice! If God's people, our dear brothers and sisters, if we can pray without ceasing, will God not hear our prayers? God is so much better than the officials of this world, is He not! I tell you, my dear brothers and sisters, God will grant you justice quickly!

2. God answered Daniel's prayer through an angel (Daniel 10:1-14)

In Daniel 10 of the Old Testament, we see what the Bible says: Daniel wanted to understand what would happen in the future (Daniel 10:12). He prayed earnestly before God, and the Bible says Daniel ate no choice food, no meat or wine touched his lips, and he did not anoint himself with oil. Daniel prayed earnestly for three weeks, twenty-one days, and then an answer came to Daniel. Let us read Daniel 10:12-14 together. (Scripture omitted here)

The Bible says that on the very first day Daniel prayed earnestly, his words were already heard by God! But the prince of the Persian kingdom resisted him for twenty-one days, until Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help. Dear brothers and sisters, God heard Daniel's prayer on the first day. How many days did Daniel pray? Daniel's prayer was heard on the first day, but how many days passed before the answer came? Why? Because in the unseen spiritual world, a prince was resisting! Dear brothers and sisters, Daniel kept praying until God's answer came to him. If you are praying for something especially important and receive no answer after praying, should you keep praying? Yes, you must keep praying! Even pray earnestly with fasting!

3. P.U.S.H. — Pray Until Something Happens!

Let me share a story. There was a man who was sleeping in his little hut at night when suddenly the room was filled with light, and God appeared to him. The Lord told him that He was giving him a task. The Lord brought him to a large rock at the door of his hut and told him to push the rock with all his might. So day after day, he obeyed. From sunrise to sunset, every day he pressed his shoulder against the rough rock and pushed with all his strength. And so it went for many years.

Every night he returned to his hut exhausted and aching all over, feeling that it had been another day of fruitless labor. Because he showed some discouragement, the evil one (Satan) decided to plant seeds of doubt in his weak mind: You have been pushing this rock for a long time, and it has not moved at all.

Thus the evil one (Satan) made him feel that this was an impossible task. That he was a failure. These thoughts made him feel even more discouraged and dejected. Satan further suggested: Why go all out on this matter? Since you are already spending time on it, just go through the motions, push with a little less force, that is enough. The weary man planned to do exactly that, but first he decided to pray to God and bring his questions before the Lord to be clarified.

"Lord," he said, "I have labored in your service for a long time, and I have put all my strength into doing what you asked me to do. Yet despite all this, the rock has not moved at all. What is wrong? Why do I fail like this?" The Lord answered with compassion: "When I asked you to serve me, you accepted. I told you your task was to push the rock with all your might, and you did. I never said I expected you to move the rock... your task was only to push. Now you come before me, having done your best and feeling you have gained nothing. But is that really true? Look at yourself: your arms are strong and powerful, your back is bronzed by the sun, how strong you are; your hands have developed calluses from long pushing, and your legs have become very sturdy. Through all this you have matured greatly, and your strength far exceeds what it was before. Indeed, you have not moved the rock. But my calling to you was to obey, to push the rock, and through this to train your faith and trust in my wisdom, and you have done all of it. Now, it is my turn to move the rock."

Many times, when we hear God's word, we try to use our own cleverness to explain what God wants. In fact, all God needs is simple faith and obedience, and He wants us to do our utmost to train the faith that can "move mountains." But know this: moving the mountain is still God's work.

When everything goes wrong, just push (P.U.S.H.); when work makes our faith sink, just push; when people do not respond as you expected, just push; when you cannot make ends meet, just push.

Conclusion

When our prayers are not heard, we can reflect: is the channel of our prayer clear, or is it blocked by sin and our own desires? Are we continuing to pray and wait in Christ Jesus, and does my prayer agree with God's will? When I pray, do I believe that the God I pray to will hear me? When your prayers align with God's will in these areas, I believe you are very close to receiving God's answer! May every brother and sister among us be heard by God in prayer and receive God's grace! Amen! Let us bow our heads and pray together!
