Believing Has Power

The centurion believed that one word from Jesus could heal his servant! Not only was his servant healed by Jesus because of his faith, but he was also commended by Jesus—even in Israel, Jesus had not found anyone with such great faith.

Some people, by faith, carried their paralyzed friend to Jesus. Not only was the paralyzed friend healed, Jesus even said to him, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”

Then there was the woman with the flow of blood who said, “If I only touch Jesus’ garment, I will be made well!” Jesus said to her, “Daughter, take heart; your faith has made you well!” And from that moment the woman was healed.

Blind Bartimaeus received sight through faith! The Canaanite woman came asking Jesus to heal her demon-possessed daughter, and heard Jesus say, “It is not right to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.” But by faith the Canaanite woman replied, “Yes, Lord, yet even the dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their masters’ table.” So Jesus healed her daughter.

What we are talking about here is the power of belief. Although believing comes with actions, and actions are indispensable, the faith behind the actions is even more indispensable! So behind these deeds, what Jesus commended was faith! Without the centurion’s faith, we cannot reproduce the healing of the centurion’s servant; without the faith of the paralyzed man’s friends, we cannot reproduce the healing of the paralyzed man.

If we have faith, Jesus said, even faith as small as a mustard seed can move mountains! And Jesus said, “If you can! All things are possible for one who believes.” Jesus’ meaning is: with Him, nothing is impossible for those who believe! Amen!

Seeing Is Believing, or Blessed Are Those Who Believe Without Seeing?

The world says, “Seeing is believing.” Jesus says, “Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.” Amen! We have not seen Jesus, yet believing in Him is blessed! We must also believe Jesus’ word—not believing because we see healing, but believing while healing has not yet happened that Jesus is the God who heals us! Amen!

The World’s Ways and the Word of God

In the youth fellowship of my former church, we often saw God’s healing. I would organize the young people of the fellowship to serve in healing meetings, and they were very enthusiastic.

Sometimes when their family members were healed in the meetings, they would ask, “Were you really healed? Do not lie!” Sometimes our brothers and sisters find it more normal to not be healed, and are surprised when healing happens! This shows our faith is still insufficient!

In the youth meetings some students studied traditional Chinese medicine. They would enthusiastically buy, boil, and deliver medicine for other brothers and sisters in the church. But even having seen and experienced healing firsthand, they still easily help others in their own accustomed ways.

We are used to helping people in the world’s way. Helping one another and loving one another is not a bad thing! But let us not forget that we have a God—with Him nothing is impossible! Amen!

We are used to the world’s ways, even though we have experienced God and seen healing! Because we live in this world—though we are Christians—we still experience difficulties, pain, even sickness and death in this world!

So questions arise within us: I am already a Christian, and I truly believe Jesus is real! Why do I still experience these problems? During the pastor’s wife’s battle with cancer, we often heard a voice asking: why do the pastor and his wife experience such things?

Everyone experiences things they do not want. Difficulty or suffering is a process; after going through it, we gain tested faith. But during the process, the feelings are not good! Yet faith does not only happen after the trial—in the process itself, we must choose whether to believe and follow God in the pain!

The Double-Slit Experiment and the Hawthorne Effect

When scientists send electrons or photons one by one through a barrier with two slits, the particles leave two parallel lines on the screen behind. After firing for a long time, however, alternating bright and dark interference fringes appear on the screen. This proves that a single particle, when passing through the double slits, behaves like a wave and interferes with itself.

Add a detector: if you place a detector beside the slits to see which slit each particle actually passes through, something wonderful happens. The fringes disappear: as soon as an “observation” occurs, the interference fringes vanish, leaving only two ordinary particle-impact lines. Wave-function collapse: this shows that microscopic particles exist in a state of probability waves until measured, and measurement collapses the wave state into a definite particle state.

The Hawthorne effect refers to the phenomenon that when a person knows they are being watched, observed, or tested, they change their behavior and perform better or more actively than usual.

Both people and particles change under observation. So when we focus on God’s word, will His word also bring change into our lives? I believe the answer is yes! The Bible says God’s word is health to all our flesh. If you believe, receive, and soak yourself in God’s word, His word will release power to heal you! Amen!

Active Following or Passive Following?

Today’s Christians are also called disciples in the Bible; one meaning of “disciples of Jesus” is those who follow Jesus. How will you and I follow Jesus today—with conscious, active following, or passive following?

The difference between active and passive learning for a student is huge. I believe the difference between actively and passively following Jesus will also show in our lives. Others may not know clearly, but we ourselves certainly know the condition of our own lives. When we actively follow Jesus’ word, our condition becomes like the disciples in the Bible—continually experiencing God’s power! When we passively follow Jesus’ word, we experience pulling and tension!

In the past, when praying for the sick in church, I often asked after prayer: “How are you now? Has your body changed compared with before the prayer?” Usually some people are not used to such questions. In fact, this helps the brothers and sisters being prayed for to examine the condition of their bodies. In this process, they inevitably think about God’s word upon themselves; if we have faith in God’s word, that faith is catalyzed and strengthened in the process! And that is why we see the sick healed.

Closing: Responding to God’s Word with Faith

We need to respond to God’s word with faith. If we focus more on God’s word, we will be covered by the power of His word! This does not mean you will never experience difficulty and suffering. It means that in every trial and hardship, you are not alone—you have the Lord Jesus and your brothers and sisters, and you have the covering of the church! Say to those around you: “Jesus loves you, you are not alone!”
