Introduction

Recently, while reviewing the sermons of our church, I noticed there is almost nothing on "idols and curses," yet this area of teaching is quite important. In Acts, the apostles said that for believing Gentiles, "idolatry" was one of the forbidden things. For a Gentile church like ours, this teaching is therefore especially important.

Scripture · Acts 15:20

But should write to them to abstain from the things polluted by idols, and from sexual immorality, and from what has been strangled, and from blood.

Scripture · Acts 21:25

But as for the Gentiles who have believed, we have sent a letter with our judgment that they should abstain from what has been sacrificed to idols, and from blood, and from what has been strangled, and from sexual immorality.

Acts 15 says: "abstain from the things polluted by idols, and from sexual immorality, and from what has been strangled, and from blood." Acts 21 says: "what has been sacrificed to idols, and from blood, and from what has been strangled, and from sexual immorality." Taken together, prohibitions related to idols make up a quarter of the list. Abstaining from things polluted by idols means Christians must not eat food offered to idols, and must not worship idols either.

I. Idolatry brings a curse

Scripture · Deuteronomy 27:15

Cursed be the man who makes a carved or cast metal image, an abomination to the LORD, a thing made by the hands of a craftsman, and sets it up in secret. And all the people shall answer and say, "Amen."

Scripture · Deuteronomy 5:9

You shall not bow down to them or serve them, for I the LORD your God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on the children to the third and the fourth generation.

Scripture · Deuteronomy 7:26

And you shall not bring an abominable thing into your house, lest you become devoted to destruction like it. You shall utterly detest and abhor it, for it is devoted to destruction.

Scripture · Deuteronomy 18:10–12

There shall not be found among you anyone who burns his son or his daughter as an offering, anyone who practices divination or tells fortunes or interprets omens, or a sorcerer, or a charmer, or a medium, or a necromancer, or one who inquires of the dead. For whoever does these things is an abomination to the LORD. And because of these abominations the LORD your God is driving them out before you.

From these passages in Deuteronomy we see that whoever makes idols certainly bears a curse; whoever bows down to idols—God visits his iniquity on him from father to son to the third and fourth generation; bringing idol-related objects into the home is also forbidden, making one devoted to destruction like the object itself; and fortune-telling, divination, sorcery, witchcraft, mediums, and spiritism are all an abomination to God.

From this we can sort out four categories: making idols, bowing down to idols, possessing objects connected with idols, and other practices connected with the spirits behind idols—all fall under "idolatry." God detests these practices, and they bring down a curse.

It is not that God deliberately curses people, but that these practices give the spirits behind them a breach to control those caught in them.

A person cannot both worship God and worship idols, for God is a jealous God. Idolatry brings a curse pursued across generations: you must not carve or bow down to idols, for God will visit the iniquity of those who worship idols (who hate God) "to the third and the fourth generation." This curse, passed from generation to generation because of ancestors’ sin, brings demonic bondage and tragedy. We call it a "generational curse."

Idolatry or disobedience to God’s word actively opens a door (gives ground) to the enemy: the devil, evil spirits, or unclean spirits cannot force anyone unless the person actively gives them an opportunity. When a person participates in idol worship, sorcery, fortune-telling, or makes covenants with false gods, he is in reality cooperating with dark powers in the spiritual realm, opening a breach (giving ground) in his life, and providing evil spirits with a lawful contract and right to dwell and take root in the family.

II. Idolatry becomes a familial curse

Once an ancestor leaves a lawful breach to the enemy through idolatry, evil spirits enter and remain in that family for a long time as a matter of course. The Bible says: from father to son, to the third and fourth generation.

The "moving house" and transfer of evil spirits: these spirits welcomed in when ancestors worshipped idols are called familial spirits. Modern anime and films romanticize demon possession—usually the host gains the power of the indwelling spirit and then begins a "cheat-code life" in the story.

Because evil spirits are spiritual beings, they desperately seek human bodies to inhabit in the material world. When an ancestor who worshipped idols dies, these spirits do not disappear; they seek blood relatives as new dwellings, transferring to descendants through opportunities such as a mother’s pregnancy, childbirth, or the death of a loved one.

Scripture · Matthew 12:43

When the unclean spirit has gone out of a person, it passes through waterless places seeking rest, but finds none.

The Bible says an unclean spirit is an evil spirit; it finds rest only in a human being, and away from people it finds no rest. So if it has the chance, it will not leave a person.

An unclean spirit will not willingly leave the host it possesses, because it treats the host’s body as its house, its home. "Unclean spirit" and "evil spirit" are two names describing evil forces that attach themselves to human bodies.

The devil, or Satan, is a fallen angel. From the biblical text, the origin of unclean and evil spirits is not clearly stated; but unclean and evil spirits long to attach themselves to people, while the devil, as a fallen angel, has no such need.

The curse brought by idol worship is connected with evil spirits or unclean spirits. These spirits learn the human weaknesses of a family and seduce through those weaknesses, causing the ensnared member to sink into them, bound and controlled by evil forces.

Evil spirits tempt and attack descendants in their weakness (such as anxiety, depression, or fear), causing particular patterns of sin (such as alcoholism, violence, sexual immorality, or gambling) or particular diseases to be passed down through generations, sometimes skipping one.

III. The breaches and bondage of idolatry

The breach of worshipping ancestors as false gods: our traditional customs lead us to worship the memorial tablets of the dead. Idolatry and ancestor worship are the two greatest bondages and breaches handed down by our ancestors, making descendants extremely vulnerable to the grip of generational curses. This is what Ephesians calls "the spirit that is now at work in the sons of disobedience" behind worldly customs.

Scripture · Ephesians 2:2

In which you once walked, following the course of this world, following the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that is now at work in the sons of disobedience.

Bondage from ignorant involvement: many people, out of ignorance, followed folk customs while growing up—fortune-telling, divination, playing with spirit-writing (ouija), even being dedicated by their parents in childhood as adopted sons or daughters of Matsu or Guanyin.

These covenant-making practices with idols provide the powers of darkness with a lawful channel to manipulate and destroy people’s lives.

IV. How do we break the familial curses of idolatry?

Although Jesus Christ already bore the curse of the law on the cross and redeemed us, for this redemption to actually take effect in a personal life by removing the curse, one must actively cooperate in spiritual cleansing:

Stage one: remove "devoted things"

Spiritual principle: evil spirits are bodiless spirits; they love to cling to objects connected with them. Keeping fortune-telling books, prayer beads, amulets, lucky cats, I-Ching and Bagua materials, or even pornographic and violent objects in the home gives evil spirits a lawful standing and right to dwell.

Practical prayer and action: before cleaning, ask for the covering and protection of the blood of Jesus; search and thoroughly discard, burn, or smash all physical objects in the home connected with idols, fortune-telling, divination, or cultic qigong.

Stage two: follow the Bible, stand in the breach and confess the sins of ancestors

Spiritual principle: based on Scripture we may stand in the breach on behalf of our blood relatives and ancestors, confessing and repenting before God, asking that the blood of Jesus would seal the lawful breach left to the devil by the sins of ancestors (such as idolatry, worship of ancestral tablets, shedding innocent blood, lust, sexual immorality, and violence).

Scripture · Leviticus 26:40

But if they confess their iniquity and the iniquity of their fathers in their treachery that they committed against me, and also in walking contrary to me.

Scripture · Nehemiah 1:6

Let your ear be attentive and your eyes open, to hear the prayer of your servant that I now pray before you day and night for the people of Israel your servants, confessing the sins of the people of Israel, which we have sinned against you. Even I and my father’s house have sinned.

Scripture · Nehemiah 9:2

And the Israelites separated themselves from all foreigners and stood and confessed their sins and the iniquities of their fathers.

Offer specific prayers of confession according to the folk customs of the region (worshipping Guanyin, Matsu, Guan Gong, the Earth God, ancestral tablets, fortune-telling, fire-walking, and so on).

Stage three: thoroughly forgive, breaking the bitter breach caused by wounds

Spiritual principle: unforgiveness, hatred, and bitterness are among the chief "lawful breaches" by which Satan gains a foothold and demons dwell in people’s lives. If we do not forgive from the heart those parents, elders, or others who have hurt us, the enemy has something to say in the spiritual realm, and the work of deliverance reaches a standstill.

Prayer of forgiveness and release: before God, loudly decide with your will to forgive the elders, parents, or others who have deeply hurt or abused you; renounce every thought of revenge or cursing them, and instead replace it with blessing.

Stage four: declare in the name of the Lord the annulment of all "agreements" with evil spirits

Spiritual principle: while growing up, because of parents’ ignorance you may have been dedicated to false gods such as Matsu or Guanyin as an "adopted son or daughter," or you yourself may have made vows or sworn oaths to certain idols, dolls, or imaginary friends. These covenants provide evil spirits with a contract to lawfully manipulate you.

Prayer breaking covenants with the enemy: declare in the name of Jesus that these covenants made privately or publicly have been fully annulled, blotted out, and broken by the blood of Jesus on the cross; declare that your body does not belong to Guanyin or any idol, but is the temple of the Holy Spirit.

Stage five: bind the strong man and loudly command the spirits to leave

Prayer binding the strong man and casting out demons: according to Matthew 12:29, "first bind the strong man"; declare in the name of Jesus Christ the victory of the cross, and command specific familial spirits (such as the spirit of alcohol, the spirit of poverty, the spirit of violence, the snake spirit of lust, the spirit of death) to leave you immediately, and declare that they may not touch the next generation of descendants.

Scripture · Matthew 12:29

Or how can someone enter a strong man’s house and plunder his goods, unless he first binds the strong man? Then indeed he may plunder his house.

Stage six: persevere in spiritual watchfulness in freedom

The secret of holding fast: after the prayer of release is finished, ask Jesus and the love of God to fill the empty places in your heart and body that were previously occupied by the evil one, and never to leave.

Training the will: practice self-control at all times; at the first sign of temptation, resist loudly in your thoughts and intentions in the name of the Lord.

A threefold cord: daily persist in steady and abundant reading of Scripture, powerful prayer, playing and singing along with hymns of the cross and praise in your home, while actively participating in small groups and the life of the local church—guarding against the enemy’s counterattack through means such as "doubt, fear, and unforgiveness."

Reflection · Closing

Idolatry and sin bring down the enemy’s curse, but Jesus Christ has borne our curse. If we only accept Jesus as the Savior of our lives, turn away from idols, discard idolatrous objects, bind evil spirits in the name of the Lord Jesus, command them to leave us and our families, and declare that we belong to Jesus Christ—and from then on follow Jesus Christ—the enemy can no longer control us.
