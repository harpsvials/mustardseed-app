Public church years2012 — 2015

A great many people were healed: an aunt living near Hexi Hospital was healed of kidney disease, another aunt in Hedong was healed of heart disease, and several more were healed of nervous deafness and chronic pain.

The DNA of the Church

In our church, we have seen God heal people of sickness. We once declared, “Every sick person who comes among us will be healed.” That word is continually being fulfilled.

The Power of Believing

Believing is powerful! So what must we believe in order to receive healing and deliverance? We must believe God’s promises about healing and deliverance.

Sickness Comes from the Thief in the Spiritual Realm

Sickness comes from the thief in the spiritual realm—the one the Bible calls “the devil.”

Scripture · John 10:9

I am the gate; whoever enters through me will be saved. They will come in and go out, and find pasture.

Scripture · John 10:10

The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy; I have come that they may have life, and have it to the full.

Teaching that comes from the Bible and remains faithful to the Bible comes from Jesus; and what comes from Jesus leads to salvation, and to health and abundance by which we are nourished.

To deny Jesus is to treat his words, or the Bible itself, as outdated teaching and then develop another belief system of your own. Teaching that denies Jesus comes from the thief, who steals, kills, and destroys our health and our finances—even destroying our spiritual lives.

If we want to receive supernatural health and prosperity in our finances, we must renounce the thief’s deceptions and renounce teachings that come from demons.

Scripture · 1 Corinthians 10:20–21

But I say that what the pagans sacrifice they sacrifice to demons, not to God, and I do not want you to be participants with demons. You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the table of the Lord and the table of demons.

Paul says that what unbelievers sacrifice is offered to demons, and you cannot have fellowship with demons. When you worship demons, you are in fellowship with them. That is why so many people live under demonic bondage, plagued by inexplicable fears or repeated accidents. We declare in the name of Jesus: “I renounce the demons I have worshipped and bowed down to.” “From now on, because I believe in Jesus, I belong to Jesus.”

Scripture · 1 John 3:8

The one who does what is sinful is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the devil’s work.

The Bible says the Son of God appeared to destroy the devil’s work! Thank the Lord—after we believe in Jesus, Jesus destroys the works of the devil in our lives.

The devil is a supernatural spirit-being; and in the Bible the word “spirit” also means “breath.” That is why in anime and supernatural films, evil demons are often shown as a cloud of black—or otherwise colored—gas. In real life, when someone is under demonic bondage, you cannot actually see color. Filmmakers give it color in order to describe it.

When Jesus comes into your life, one of the graces he brings is the power to drive out the devil’s authority—and he has entrusted this ministry to the church. Many people think deliverance takes only a minute, but in truth, like healing, deliverance is a process: sometimes short, sometimes long. The difference lies in whether the conditions the devil feeds on have been dealt with.

The devil clings to people usually through three things: bitterness toward others, sexual immorality, and idolatry. Bitterness must be forgiven and released; immorality must be repented of and changed in behavior; idols must be renounced. Then the devil’s bondage is broken more easily, and the sickness the devil brought is healed more readily.
