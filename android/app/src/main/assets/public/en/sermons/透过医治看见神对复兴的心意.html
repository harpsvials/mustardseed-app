Why do we always share testimonies first in every gathering?

Why do we always begin every healing gathering by sharing some testimonies? Because we long to kindle the desire of the brothers and sisters through shared testimony. The Scripture says, “those who testify to Jesus have the Spirit of prophecy” (Rev. 19:10). This means God can speak prophecies over your life through testimony—God can do it again.

Healing testimonies from Hengyang

On April 2, 2018, Teacher Ye and I led a fasting prayer meeting in Hengyang. Before I began that afternoon, Teacher Ye had already built a wonderful spiritual atmosphere in the whole room, and the brothers and sisters were earnestly praying and seeking God. I shared testimonies from our church’s half-night prayer in March: “someone was filled with the Holy Spirit as they hungered”; “someone shared that the Holy Spirit led her to see the Lord Jesus and encouraged her to walk boldly toward Him”; and at one early-morning prayer meeting in March, a sister danced in the Spirit to praise God. The brothers and sisters in Hengyang hungered greatly and met God and the working of His great power in the prayer meeting.

1

Herniated lumbar disc: several brothers and sisters were healed on the spot.

2

Numb hands and feet: healed.

3

Uterine fibroid: a sister shared that during prayer a power suddenly entered her abdomen, and what had felt uncomfortable became completely comfortable.

4

Restored hearing: a brother wearing a hearing aid had been completely deaf in both ears; during prayer his hearing began to return, and he estimated about 10% was restored. We encouraged him to begin thanking God for that 10%.

5

Lump on the knee: a sister had a painful hard lump on her knee; after prayer the lump began to soften and the pain greatly lessened.

6

Stroke and brain infarction: two patients made remarkable recoveries—one could walk back and forth on the platform, and one could raise and swing an arm up and down, though he had not been able to raise it in church before.

7

Burden lifted: several others shared that the burdens on them vanished during the prayer meeting. One sister said she had felt as if a great stone pressed on her head and chest, but in prayer God lifted all her burdens and she felt completely light.

The miracles and healings in this gathering came through the Lord Jesus’s mercy and love, and were also inseparable from the hunger of the brothers and sisters. The whole room hungered and sought earnestly. Moreover, as testimonies were shared midway, they encouraged one another into deeper hunger, so that more people were touched by the Holy Spirit and fell under the Spirit. A great awe swept through the room—crying out over sin and righteousness, and joy in the Holy Spirit filled the whole place; one serving co-worker also received a very strong, fiery filling of the Holy Spirit.

Testimony is your inheritance

Bible · Psalm 119:111

Your statutes are my heritage forever; they are the joy of my heart.

In other words, testimony becomes our inheritance. In Joshua 24:31, Israel served the Lord throughout Joshua’s lifetime and all the days of the elders who outlived him, who had known all the works the Lord had done for Israel. But in the days of the judges, when testimony of God was no longer passed on, Scripture describes Judges 21:25: “in those days Israel had no king; everyone did as they saw fit.” I know most people leave their estate to their descendants, but have you never considered that testimony is your inheritance? The Israelites who experienced God’s supernatural power at the Exodus—when their descendants no longer remembered God’s deeds, they acted wickedly. Will you not give a testimony, that testimony may become your inheritance?

I want to tell you: I will not only give a testimony, I will also tell my descendants in detail how I came to know Jesus and how I have experienced Him. I must pass on this spiritual inheritance to my descendants. So I likewise encourage you: testimony is your inheritance, and when you testify, the Spirit of prophecy is present, so that those who hear receive the power of God, and healing and deliverance come.

So we need to testify. Testimony is like a notary witnessing the dispute between the kingdom of light and the kingdom of darkness; and when the notary declares that this healing belongs to you because Jesus has already paid the price, the testimony has the force of a notarized deed, and it becomes easier to hold fast to your healing. I know many people are unwilling to testify, because most of us have a habit called reserve—we are reluctant to express ourselves before others. But you must know that testimony is your inheritance. In Revelation 12:11: “they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony…” When you begin to testify, you can overcome the devil through the power of the blood of the Lamb in your testimony. The devil can no longer steal your healing so easily, because it has been notarized. Some people often receive healing at healing meetings, then about half a year later the illness returns; but the verse in Revelation tells us that the blessing and power stirred up by testimony can overcome the works of the devil.

Pastor Xun Guang shared at a Heavenly Culture conference in February 2018: “Over the city of Reading, where Bethel Church is, there is an open heaven; it is easier to experience God in that city.” Though Bethel Church is in a very remote city of fewer than a hundred thousand people, how has it influenced and shaken the whole world? It not only draws twenty-five hundred students every year, coming with their whole families to be equipped, but also sends out tens of thousands of revivalists who continually shake the nations (see the Asia for Jesus website). And I believe God’s heart is that signs and wonders should not be rare accidents, but the most natural experience of a Christian. I believe God longs, through healing and deliverance, to reveal His boundless mercy and to reveal His heart to bring revival to the whole earth!

I call on more young brothers and sisters to hunger to enter into the Holy Spirit, to hunger more deeply for God’s Spirit, and to seek that God would revive us through the Spirit—so that we may enter in and begin to see the mighty works of God become the common experience of our church; and so that we may stir up more people to seek God, to pursue spiritual revival, and to enter the mighty presence of God—so that we not only experience God ourselves, but also lead others into God’s power.

God wants to heal this broken generation

All along I have deeply believed that it is God’s heart to heal this broken generation—whether the healing of physical sickness or the healing of inner wounds. I believe this is God’s call to the church: “Go, preach the gospel of the kingdom, heal the sick, drive out demons!” I believe God’s full gospel must include healing. And God’s supernatural healing can awaken a person’s hunger and obedience toward God. Once a person encounters God’s power and bows down, it is no longer so hard. Even now I greatly long that God would continually reveal His mighty presence in every gathering!

Yet I also believe God’s heart is not only healing; He longs, through healing and deliverance, to kindle the flame of revival within us, to burn our lives for Jesus Christ, and to let the true light of Christ shine into this world—to shine on those in the valley of the shadow of death, that people may see God’s power and awaken the revival within! Let the whole earth see the glory of God!

We owe this generation a chance to meet God’s power

Bible · Mark 16:16–18

Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. And these signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will drive out demons; they will speak in new tongues; they will pick up snakes with their hands; and when they drink deadly poison, it will not hurt them at all; they will place their hands on sick people, and they will get well.

In the past, the professors and pastors at the seminary I attended all told me not to pray for the sick; they tried to convince me that the church no longer needs God’s healing today. Yet I tell you, God is unchanging. In the past He healed the sick out of mercy, and today He will still, out of mercy, use us who believe to heal the sick.

The Scripture says that signs will accompany those who believe—driving out demons in the name of Jesus Christ, speaking in new tongues, laying hands on the sick that they may be healed—it does not say only certain people. So you must know that we who believe should reveal the healing power of God. I love a saying of Pastor Bill Johnson: we owe this generation an encounter with God. If you can receive it, you should engrave the word of God on your heart—the healing power God has reserved is within you! And you need to believe and receive, and by faith pray for the sick!

So you must not only long to receive healing from God; you must also long for the fire of revival to kindle your life, and live out the life that God calls you to live.

The Spirit of God brings the power of God

Bible · Matthew 12:28

But if it is by the Spirit of God that I drive out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.

Bible · Luke 11:20

But if it is by the finger of God that I drive out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.

From the contrast between these two verses you can see that the Spirit of God carries the power of God! And you need the Holy Spirit to fill you continually, so that there is within you an ample supply of God’s mighty power to serve, for the sake of Jesus Christ, the people God places around you.

Speaking in tongues builds up yourself

Bible · 1 Corinthians 14:4

Anyone who speaks in a tongue edifies themselves.

Bible · 1 Corinthians 14:2

For anyone who speaks in a tongue does not speak to people but to God. Indeed, no one understands them; they utter mysteries by the Spirit.

Bible · 1 Corinthians 14:14

For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is fruitless.

The Spirit of God brings the power of God, and speaking in tongues is God building you up in your spirit through a mysterious way. This building up needs faith to set it in motion, because your mind cannot understand what the tongues are saying. People need faith whenever they do something they do not fully understand or are not sure about. And this faith sets in motion the effect of the gift of tongues building you up in the spirit. It is also faith that lets you receive the power of God.

Speaking in tongues is one of the signs of being filled with the Holy Spirit. In being filled with the Spirit and speaking in tongues, the presence of God is brought down. Each of us has the ability Jesus gave; the difference is that some live it out and some do not. Speaking in tongues builds you up with secret words, letting the power of God within you be revealed. Speaking in tongues is like drawing water with a pump—pressing it again and again may seem monotonous and repetitive, yet it draws up from the inner well a spring of living water, bringing the power of life.

Testimonies from revival history

In the Bible, wherever Jesus went there was healing. Revivals in church history were always accompanied by healing conferences and the supernatural manifestation of signs and wonders—including John Wesley and George Whitefield of the Reformation, as well as Finney, Moody, Kathryn Kuhlman, John Lake, and others. The gatherings of those who led revival in church history were full of supernatural healing and deliverance, being filled with the Holy Spirit, slain in the Spirit, and being carried away in the Spirit. Of course, many books do not record the scenes of their gatherings; they record only their sermons and biographies, deleting the outward works of the Spirit, simply because those authors did not recognize the outward manifestation of the Spirit as the source of revival.

There is a book on the history of church revival that tells of an igniter of revival named John Lake. He said, “I believe I am the person who has hungered most for God in all history.” What he pursued was “more,” not merely an interesting experience when the meeting ended. He longed to live out the way of life of the apostles of the first century—a longing so deep it ached.

In 1908 in Africa, at the first meeting, Lake prayed that the Holy Spirit would be poured out on more than five hundred Zulu people, and revival broke out immediately. While preaching in South Africa, a man disrupted the meeting; Lake pointed at him and said “Sit down,” and the man fell as though struck and lay on the ground for three hours. When he recovered he said, “Something hit me and passed through my body; I thought I had been shot.” Serving in Africa, Lake saw hundreds of thousands of people healed.

Lake returned to the United States in 1912 and in 1913 founded the Healing Prayer Room in Spokane. Within five or six years, a hundred thousand medically documented healing cases occurred there. According to the U.S. government’s statistics at the time, “Pastor Lake, through divine healing, made Spokane the healthiest city in the world.”

By 1924 he had planted forty churches across the nation. Out of Lake’s ministry arose a leader, Gordon Lindsay, who founded “Christ for the Nations,” now present in more than 120 countries; there is also “Christ for the Nations Institute,” which has trained more than forty thousand students.

Kyle Pierce inherited the mantle and continues the healing-prayer-room ministry Lake founded. There are now more than three thousand healing prayer rooms, spread across more than seventy-three countries.

Who has ruled that only special people can be revived by God and carry His power? Is it not those who believe? God does not show favoritism. If, in church history, these revivals were brought through ordinary, common people, who says your life cannot be kindled, cannot be revived, cannot carry the power of God? Cry out to God, people of God! Pray with your understanding and also in the Spirit; pray in your everyday language and also in tongues. Now, from that well of God’s presence within you, press out through your praying in tongues the living spring of life and power.
