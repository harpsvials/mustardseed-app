Dec 2008Changsha · First time

A senior from Bible school took me to preach at a small church in Changsha. On the bus I suddenly felt a deep longing to pray for the sick and to see them healed. We were on a bumpy minibus; as it jolted, I silently prayed to God: “Lord, if it is You who wants me to pray for people, let the sick come to me so I may pray for them.”

That day I preached, and the senior prayed for the sick. After the service, an elderly woman came to me and asked me to pray for her younger sister. First came the headache; after praying twice, she said her head no longer hurt. Actually, at the time I did not yet have faith that her headache was completely gone.

The woman went on to say her sister was also deaf in one ear. I felt a little nervous, so I asked them to come with me to a quiet spot by the stairs where no one would notice. I prayed for her ear in the name of Jesus, about twice; she said her hearing had fully returned and she could clearly hear us speak.

After I had repeatedly confirmed her hearing was restored, I asked her to go into the church and give her testimony to glorify God. After she testified, her sister said again: my sister also cannot see clearly. I prayed about five times; after the first few prayers she was not healed. I asked her: “If God could heal your ear, can He not also heal your eyes?” She answered: “Yes!” Once more I led all the brothers and sisters in prayer for her, and the white cloudy film over her pupil completely disappeared. I later learned it had been a cataract!

2012Full-time ministry

After I began working full-time at the church, the sister who usually led the prayer meeting once asked me, before a business trip, to lead a prayer meeting for her. The brothers and sisters told her I had led it well. When she went on another trip, she asked me to lead the prayer meeting again.

During that prayer meeting, as I prayed with my eyes closed, I saw a heart circled by a ring. I declared that God would heal any brother or sister among us with heart disease. A woman surnamed Jiang testified that her body felt as though ants were biting her, and her heart was healed. It was 2012; the last time I saw this woman was in 2021, when I visited a patient. She said that in the past her heart ached whenever the weather turned stormy, but since God healed her, for ten years her heart has never acted up again.

At the same meeting, a sister who led worship described how she had suffered pelvic pain; as she prayed, a warm flow passed through her and the pain vanished. Several others also testified that they had received God’s healing.

Shuxiangli · NorthSmall groups & meetings

Once, at a small group in Shuxiangli, a woman surnamed Zeng had a steel rod inside her leg and was in great pain; another sister, Wen, brought her to the group on a motorcycle. The doctor said that at her age such pain was normal. We prayed for her in the group, and her whole body began to tremble. I asked her: “Are you trembling from pain?” She said: “No, it is not pain; my body feels very comfortable.” I had her sit on a chair and call me when the trembling stopped. After it stopped, I helped her up to walk; her leg no longer hurt at all, and she could walk on her own.

Once, at a meeting in the North District, I shared that “when we turn back, God heals us, so that our eyes may see and our ears may hear.” I called those with hearing problems to come forward; seven or eight people came up, and six or seven of them fully recovered their hearing and were healed. Also, those who could not straighten their fingers could straighten them, and those who could not bend them could bend them; lumbar muscle strain was healed, and a herniated disc was healed.

Once, after morning prayer at church, an elderly woman stopped me and asked me to pray for a woman named Pan Mingxiu, whose hearing in both ears was poor; when I spoke to her face to face, she could not make out what I was saying. As I prayed, I placed my fingers on her ears and commanded in the name of Jesus that the deaf spirit leave her. After a simple rebuke in the name of Jesus, when we spoke, she could hear.

In that same year, there were more than ten other unnamed brothers and sisters whose hearing was restored; all through praying in the name of Jesus and commanding the deaf spirit to leave, their ears were opened. We can see that resisting the devil behind sickness in the name of Jesus is a great grace and right for every Christian. I believe that when you begin to pray in the name of Jesus, you will begin to experience the power of that name.

2014.09.21Qingshuitang, North

On 2014.09.21 at Qingshuitang in the North District, many people said they had been healed as we prayed! After the meeting, many more came asking for individual prayer, including people with leg pain, cataracts, glaucoma, and optic nerve atrophy.

Several with cataracts reported improvement; I did not have full faith to confirm they were healed, but it was Jesus who healed. One woman also had a steel rod in her leg and said the pain was gone, and she was so happy she jumped up! Most with leg pain were healed and went home rejoicing, though one still felt pain on the way out, and I do not know the reason.

After praying for the woman with optic nerve atrophy, I told her to wait with her eyes closed, but she opened them and told me she could not see! I prayed for her once more and told her to slowly open her eyes, cover her right eye, and look with her left; astonishingly, her left eye could see—and her left eye was the one with optic nerve atrophy! Our God is truly a mighty God!

2014.10-11Morning prayer & Sister J

At the morning prayer meeting on 2014.10.2, two deaf people were healed by Jesus. One deaf person had only a little hearing in one ear; that ear was healed. The other deaf person had a hearing aid in one ear with some hearing, while the other ear was dead and the aid had broken; after prayer, the hearing aid was removed and both ears heard. Four others—one with an inflamed ear and three with tinnitus, with ringing in their ears—after prayer the ringing stopped and the inflamed ear improved. One more person with pain in the back meridians and leg pain was completely well.

In 2014, a woman called Sister J bled in her urine for over twenty days; the central hospital could find no cause. I led her to repent of her past idol worship; at that moment a surge rose in her belly and finally left through her nose. On November 02 of the same year, I met Sister J at church; she excitedly grabbed my hand and told me God had healed her illness. As she took her medicine, the pills slipped from her hand several times; the last one fell under the sofa and could not be picked up. Her husband, who had not yet believed, said: “Your God does not want you to take medicine!” So she stopped taking it, and God healed her bleeding through her prayer of repentance.

2015-2016Church ministry

At the healing meeting on 2015.5.1, Sister Zu’s leg pain was healed, and she also received healing for kidney disease. Around National Day that year, a hospital examination confirmed that her decades-long kidney disease was completely healed.

In 2016, the pastor of a nearby regional church invited us to their new church, where I preached on the Lord’s Day and prayed for the sick for healing. Many were healed again, but I noticed that when patients laid hands on themselves and prayed for themselves, they too experienced healing and deliverance. I was especially struck by a patient who had returned from America; she had attended healing meetings there and her body pains had left, but her own nerve deafness had not been healed. In this meeting, she laid hands on herself and received prayer, and her nerve deafness was healed.

2018-2019Various healings

On 2018.4.10, Sister Chen shared that her cervical spine had been healed; she herself could not believe it at first, but after repeated confirmation over several days, it was truly well.

Around 2018, a seeker at the Hetai church was demon-possessed; I led him to repent and confess his sins, the unclean spirit manifested, and I cast it out in the name of Jesus (details omitted).

On 2019.3.26, after a car accident, Sister Xia was found to have too wide a gap in her back spine; as she prayed she fell to the floor and felt the discs of her back spine grow warm, shift, and be restored to health. The tendon connecting her leg bone to its socket had been removed due to muscle fibrosis; when she walked she could feel the bone slip out of the socket, but after that same prayer, though she tried many times, it no longer slipped out.

On 2019.9.27, Sister Jin shared that after giving birth she had suffered lower back pain and back pain for about five months. After the first healing prayer meeting the pain disappeared; she felt it should not be so and thought it was only temporary, and the next day the pain truly returned. At the prayer meeting the following week, after receiving prayer the pain left at once; after several days of repeated confirmation that it was truly gone, she shared this testimony.
